<Lead>
For centuries, the Arabic terms *Islam* and *Muslim* were treated across global translations as exclusive proper nouns—tribal club memberships denoting a particular ethnic and cultural bloc. The decision in modern Quranic translations to render these terms into direct English (*Submission* and *Submitter*) represented a decisive theological breakthrough that dissolved sectarian barriers.
</Lead>

## The Problem of Untranslated Arabic Labels

When the Arabic word *Muslim* (مُسْلِم) is left untranslated in an English sentence, it functions syntactically as a proprietary sectarian label. Non-Muslims view it as an alien cultural identity, while traditionalists treat it as an exclusive franchise inherited by birth.

Yet in the Quran, *Islam* is not a proprietary name invented in 7th-century Arabia; it is an active description of total devotion and surrender to the One God:

<Verse
  reference="Quran 3:19"
  text="The only religion approved by God is 'Submission.' Ironically, those who received the scripture disputed among themselves only after knowledge had come to them, due to jealousy and rivalry."
/>

## Abraham: The Original Submitter

The Quran explicitly states that Abraham—who lived long before the Torah, the Gospel, or the Quran—was the father of this religion and the one who originally named the believers:

<Verse
  reference="Quran 22:78"
  text="Strive in the cause of God as you ought to strive. He has chosen you and placed no hardship upon you in religion—the religion of your father Abraham. He is the one who named you 'Submitters' originally and in this scripture..."
/>

<PullQuote attribution="PROVENANCE RULE">
Translating 'Muslim' to 'Submitter' is not an ideological innovation; it is the restoration of descriptive precision over sectarian tribalism.
</PullQuote>

## Unifying Monotheists Beyond Cultural Borders

By translating the root *S-L-M* into *Submission* and *Submitter*, the Quran's universal guarantee in Surat Al-Baqarah becomes transparently evident:

<Verse
  reference="Quran 2:62"
  text="Surely, those who believe, those who are Jewish, the Christians, and the converts; anyone who (1) believes in God, and (2) believes in the Last Day, and (3) leads a righteous life, will receive their recompense from their Lord. They have nothing to fear, nor will they grieve."
/>
