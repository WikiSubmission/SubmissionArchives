## The Orthography Blunder: Modern vs. Uthmani Spelling

A central objection raised by MFG involves the counting of the letter *Alif* in initialed surahs like Surat Maryam (Surah 19) and Surat Ya-Sin (Surah 36). MFG frequently utilizes modern Cairo printings with dagger alifs and vowel diacritics, claiming the counts "do not add up."

However, Dr. Rashad Khalifa's counts are based on the original **Uthmani Rasm** (the consonantal skeleton preserved since the 7th century). When the physical text of the early Qurans (such as the Tashkent, Topkapi, and early hijazi folios) is examined:

- Long vowels written defectively without dagger alifs adhere strictly to the exact 19-based integer counts.
- The initial *Nun* (ن) in Surat Al-Qalam (Surah 68) is written out phonetically in the earliest Qurans as *Nun-Waw-Nun* (ن ون), yielding an exact total of $133 = 19 \times 7$.

## The 9:128-129 Double Standard

MFG asserts that excising verses 9:128-129 was an "arbitrary deletion" invented to force numerical patterns. This claim collapses under classical Islamic scholarship:

1. **Hadith Attestation**: Classical chronicles (*Sahih Al-Bukhari*, Kitab Fada'il Al-Qur'an #4986) explicitly record that Zayd ibn Thabit found 9:128-129 with only *one single man* (Khuzaima ibn Thabit), directly violating the requirement for two independent witnesses established for every other verse.
2. **Mathematical Purification**: The inclusion of these two verses disrupts 10+ independent interlocking mathematical systems (including the total count of the word *Allah*, the sum of verse numbers, and total verse counts).
3. **Forensic Resolution**: The excision of the two false verses restores the total number of Quranic verses to $6,346 = 19 \times 334$.
