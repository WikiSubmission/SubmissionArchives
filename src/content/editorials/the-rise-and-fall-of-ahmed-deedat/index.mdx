<Lead>
In 1980, the Islamic Propagation Centre International (IPCI) in Durban, South Africa, published a short booklet titled *Al-Qur'an: The Ultimate Miracle*. Authored by Ahmed Deedat, the work distributed the 19-based mathematical structure of the Quran to millions across the globe. Yet within a decade, under intense pressure from traditionalist clerical institutions, Deedat publicly retracted his defense of the code, choosing compromise over evidence.
</Lead>

This editorial examines the historical record preserved in the Submission Archives, comparing Deedat’s documented public trajectory against the scriptural prophecy recorded in Surah 74:11–29.

---

## 1. The Proclamation of the Miracle

Following Dr. Rashad Khalifa’s 1974 discovery and subsequent publications (*The Computer Speaks*, *Miracle of the Quran: Significance of the Mysterious Alphabets*), Ahmed Deedat was among the first global Islamic speakers to recognize the transformative power of the mathematical proof.

In 1980, Deedat adapted Dr. Khalifa’s findings into *Al-Qur'an: The Ultimate Miracle*, widely circulating it during his public debates across the United States, Europe, and the Middle East.<Ref id="1" />

<PullQuote attribution="Historical Record">
The mathematical composition of the Quran was proclaimed to millions before orthodox opposition organized against it.
</PullQuote>

## 2. The Retreat Under Pressure

As traditionalist scholars and clerical bodies began rejecting the implication of the mathematical proof—specifically that the Quran is fully detailed and self-sufficient without extraneous traditions—Deedat faced growing threats to his funding and institutional standing.

By the late 1980s, Deedat ceased distributing the booklet, distanced himself from the mathematical code, and publicly aligned with the traditional establishment.

<Verse chapter={74} verses="11-16">
Leave Me to deal with the one I created as an individual. I provided him with abundant wealth. And children to witness. I made everything easy for him. Yet, he stubbornly expects Me to give more. By no means! He stubbornly refused to accept Our revelations.
</Verse>

## 3. The Prophetic Realization in Surah 74

Surah 74 (*Al-Muddath-thir*) contains the explicit mathematical sign of the Quran in verse 30: *"Over it is Nineteen."* Immediately preceding this sign, verses 11 through 29 describe a specific individual endowed with global reach and wealth who witnesses the proof, calculates his social standing, turns away in pride, and claims it is merely human invention:

<Verse chapter={74} verses="21-26">
Then he calculated. Then he frowned and looked away. Then he turned arrogant and said: 'This is nothing but traditional magic. This is nothing but human speech.' I will commit him to retribution (Saqar).
</Verse>

In 1996, Deedat suffered a severe stroke that left him with locked-in syndrome—fully conscious but completely paralyzed and unable to speak for the remaining nine years of his life, until his death in 2005.<Ref id="2" />

## 4. The Lesson for the Archive

The historical trajectory of Ahmed Deedat serves as an immutable witness to the principle that truth cannot be compromised for popularity or tradition. The primary records, letters, and audio recordings remain preserved in the archive as evidence for all generations.
