<Lead>
A recurring polemical trope attempts to divide Submitters into two artificially polarized camps: those who claim to follow "only the Quran" by discarding the messenger who unveiled its mathematical structure, and those accused of following "the messenger" as a human authority. In the light of scripture, this dichotomy is entirely false.
</Lead>

## The Sole Function of a Messenger

The Quran repeatedly defines the exact constitutional boundary of messengership: a messenger is not an independent source of religious legislation, nor the center of a personality cult. The sole responsibility of a messenger is the **unambiguous delivery and clarification of God's word**:

<Verse
  reference="Quran 5:99"
  text="The sole duty of the messenger is to deliver the message. God knows what you declare and what you conceal."
/>

<Verse
  reference="Quran 24:54"
  text="Say, 'Obey God, and obey the messenger.' If they turn away, then he is responsible only for his obligations, and you are responsible for your obligations. If you obey him, you will be guided. The sole duty of the messenger is to deliver clearly."
/>

## Why Obeying the Messenger Means Upholding the Quran

When Dr. Rashad Khalifa proclaimed the message of Submission, what was his central decree? It was the absolute, unconditional rejection of all extraneous sources (Hadith, Sunnah, clerical decrees) and the exclusive adherence to **Quran Alone**:

<Verse
  reference="Quran 6:114"
  text="Shall I seek other than God as a source of law, when He has revealed to you this book fully detailed?..."
/>

<PullQuote attribution="PROVENANCE RULE">
You cannot obey a messenger whose message is 'Follow the Quran Alone' by elevating the messenger into a separate source of religious law.
</PullQuote>

## Resolving the Polemical Trap

Those who assert "I follow the Quran, not Rashad" to justify ignoring the mathematical proofs of Code 19 or the excision of 9:128-129 fall into the exact trap warned about in Surat Al-Muddathir (74:31). Acknowledging the empirical evidence brought by a messenger is not worshiping the man—it is verifying the signature of God Almighty embedded in His text.

## Conclusion

The message and the messenger are in complete harmony:
- **The Scripture** is the eternal, immutable source of divine law.
- **The Messenger** was the instrument chosen to purify the text and demonstrate its mathematical preservation to modern humanity.

Submission Archives preserves both the text and the historical documentation to keep this foundational balance clear for all time.
