<Lead>
A recurring polemical charge leveled against the Submitters community asserts that studying, upholding, and referencing Dr. Rashad Khalifa's authorized translation, appendices, and recorded lectures is equivalent to creating a "new Hadith and Sunnah." Critics frame a sharp binary: one must either follow "the Quran alone" as an isolated text or be labeled a "Rashadian" trapped in a personality cult. In the light of Quranic theology and historical record, this dichotomy is entirely artificial.
</Lead>

This monograph examines the constitutional role of God's messengers in scripture, the distinction between unverified sectarian hearsay and authenticated messenger explanations, the psychological trap of subjective "Quranism," and why true obedience to God's messenger is inseparable from absolute devotion to God Alone.

---

## 1. The Manufactured Dilemma

The polemic typically proceeds along three assertions:
1. That a messenger is merely a passive physical courier whose role ends the moment scripture is delivered, possessing no teaching, clarifying, or administrative authority.
2. That anyone who studies or abides by the messenger's published explanations, footnotes, subtitles, and recorded sermons is substituting a mortal man for scripture and committing *shirk* (idolatry).
3. That early statements made by Dr. Khalifa emphasizing "Quran alone" in debate contexts stand in contradiction to his later declarations commanding obedience to the Messenger of the Covenant.

To evaluate these claims, one must turn to the Quran's own internal definitions of messengership, divine law, and the mechanism through which God tests believers across every generation.<Ref id="1" />

## 2. The Constitutional Function: Messengers as Appointed Teachers

The assertion that messengers possess no explanatory or teaching role is directly refuted by the Quran. God repeatedly defines the prophetic and messenger mission as an active pedagogical office:

<Verse chapter={62} verses="2">
He is the One who sent to the gentiles a messenger from among them, to recite to them His revelations, purify them, and teach them the scripture and wisdom, whereas before this they had gone far astray.
</Verse>

<Verse chapter={2} verses="151">
(Blessings) such as the sending of a messenger from among you to recite our revelations to you, purify you, and teach you the scripture and wisdom, and to teach you what you never knew.
</Verse>

The Arabic verb *yu'allimuhum* ("teaches them") establishes that scripture is not dropped into a cultural void for every reader to interpret through individual whim. The messenger is explicitly commissioned to **teach the scripture and wisdom**, to **purify** the community from accumulated innovations, and to **clarify** disputes:

<Verse chapter={16} verses="44">
...And we sent down to you this message, to clarify for the people what was sent down to them, that they may reflect.
</Verse>

<Verse chapter={16} verses="64">
We have revealed this scripture to you, to point out for them what they dispute, and to provide guidance and mercy for people who believe.
</Verse>

If a messenger's explanations and clarifications were meant to be discarded as irrelevant personal opinions, God would not have defined the office as one of clarification and teaching. The divine system utilizes human messengers to embody, demonstrate, and explain the revelation (**75:19**).

## 3. The Divine Command: "Obey the Messenger" (*Atee'u Al-Rasul*)

Over twenty times, the Quran commands believers to obey the messenger directly alongside God:

<Verse chapter={4} verses="80">
Whoever obeys the messenger has obeyed GOD. As for those who turn away, we did not send you as their guardian.
</Verse>

<Verse chapter={24} verses="54">
Say, "Obey GOD, and obey the messenger." If they refuse, then he is responsible for his obligations, and you are responsible for your obligations. If you obey him, you will be guided. The sole duty of the messenger is to deliver (the message).
</Verse>

Throughout scripture, every messenger dispatched to humanity makes the exact same constitutional demand upon the believers:
* **Noah** (26:108, 26:110, 71:3): *"You shall reverence God and obey me."*
* **Hūd** (26:126, 26:131): *"You shall reverence God and obey me."*
* **Sāleh** (26:144, 26:150): *"You shall reverence God and obey me."*
* **Lot** (26:163): *"You shall reverence God and obey me."*
* **Shu'aib** (26:179): *"You shall reverence God and obey me."*
* **Jesus** (43:63, 3:50): *"I bring to you wisdom, and to clarify some of the matters in which you dispute. You shall reverence God and obey me."*
* **Aaron** (20:90): *"Your only Lord is the Most Gracious, so follow me, and obey my commands."*

If "obeying the messenger" meant nothing more than "reading the text of scripture independently while ignoring the messenger's instructions," then the distinct divine command to obey the messenger would be entirely redundant. 

Obeying the messenger is not an act of subservience to a mortal human; it is the concrete vehicle through which a believer demonstrates loyalty and submission to **God**, who appointed that messenger with clear, verifiable proofs (**3:81**).

<PullQuote attribution="THE PRINCIPLE OF OBEDIENCE">
We do not follow a messenger because of personal allegiance to a mortal man, but because of absolute submission to God Almighty, who commanded us to obey the messenger He sent.
</PullQuote>

## 4. The False Equivalence: Messenger Teachings vs. Traditional Hadith

The core error of the polemic lies in equating Dr. Khalifa's authorized writings with traditional Sunni and Shia Hadith collections. A structural comparison reveals that they are polar opposites in origin, substance, and purpose:

| Attribute | Traditional Hadith & Sunnah | Dr. Rashad Khalifa's Teachings |
|---|---|---|
| **Provenance** | Third-hand oral lore compiled 200–300 years after Muhammad's death via unverifiable transmission chains (*isnad*). | First-hand, contemporaneous, verified writings, published translations, and video/audio recordings preserved directly in the archive. |
| **Doctrinal Substance** | Fabricates **new religious laws, prohibitions, and rituals** (e.g., stoning to death, killing apostates, banning music, altering the Shahada). | Establishes **zero new religious laws**. Strictly reinforces Quranic prohibitions and exposes unauthorized additions. |
| **Theological Goal** | Erects human idols alongside God, praises the prophet incessantly, and promises **intercession** on the Day of Judgment. | Devotes 100% of worship, praise, and devotion to **GOD ALONE** (39:45), utterly demolishing the myth of intercession (21:28). |
| **Relation to Scripture** | Claims to "judge" and overrule the Quran (*Al-Sunnah taqdi 'ala al-Kitab*). | Subordinates everything to the Quran, validating its divine authorship through empirical mathematical structure (74:30). |

Traditional Hadith was rejected not merely because of historical unreliability, but because it functions as an **unauthorized second source of divine legislation** (**6:114, 45:6**). Dr. Khalifa's appendices, footnotes, and lectures do not legislate; they serve as the authorized pedagogical exposition of the Quran's existing text delivered by the Messenger of the Covenant (**3:81**).<Ref id="2" />

## 5. "Quran Alone" vs. "My Ego Alone": The Trap of Subjective Quranism

When individuals reject the messenger's authorized explanations in the name of "Quran Alone," they do not arrive at an objective, unified understanding of scripture. Instead, they inevitably replace the messenger's guidance with **their own subjective ego**:

<Verse chapter={25} verses="43">
Have you seen the one who took his desire as his god? Then would you be over him a guardian?
</Verse>

<Verse chapter={45} verses="23">
Have you noted the one who makes his ego his god? Consequently, GOD sends him astray, despite his knowledge, seals his hearing and his heart, and places a veil on his eyes. Who then can guide him after GOD? Will you not take heed?
</Verse>

Without the baseline framework delivered through the messenger, subjective "Quranism" splinters into endless theological chaos:
* Individuals invent arbitrary interpretations of Arabic roots to alter the Contact Prayers (Salat), reducing them to 3, 2, or 0 daily prayers, or reinterpreting prayer as abstract "mental study."
* Fundamental Quranic practices—such as Zakat, fasting, and pilgrimage—are redefined according to personal convenience.
* Every individual becomes their own self-appointed prophet, asserting that their private opinion is "the Quran."

Following what you *personally imagine* the Quran says is not following the Quran alone—it is following your **ego**. True adherence to the Quran requires accepting the objective divine meaning clarified by the messenger whom God supported with overwhelming, verifiable proofs.

## 6. The Scriptural Precedent: No Messenger Ever Misled Anyone

Throughout the entire Quran, the condemned in the Hereafter lament the sources that led them astray:

<Verse chapter={25} verses="27-28">
The day will come when the transgressor will bite his hands (in anguish) and say, "Alas, I wish I had followed the path with the messenger. Alas, woe to me, I wish I did not take that person as a friend!"
</Verse>

<Verse chapter={33} verses="66-67">
The day their faces are turned over in the Hellfire, they will say, "Oh, we wish we had obeyed GOD, and obeyed the messenger." And they will say, "Our Lord, we obeyed our masters and leaders, and they misled us from the right path."
</Verse>

The Quran catalogues the exact agents of spiritual ruin across history:
* **Tyrants**: *"Thus, Pharaoh misled his people; he did not guide them"* (**20:79**).
* **Self-Appointed Innovators**: *"We have put your people to the test after you left, but the Samarian misled them"* (**20:85**).
* **Scholars and Clerics**: Taking religious leaders as lords beside God (**9:31, 7:38**).
* **Deviant Companions and Jinns**: Leading multitudes astray (**41:29, 28:63**).

**In the entire Quran, there is not a single instance, verse, or precedent where anyone was misled by following and obeying God's messenger.** God explicitly guarantees: *"If you obey him, you will be guided"* (**24:54**).

## 7. The Infallibility and Intercession Strawman

Critics frequently allege that Submitters view Dr. Khalifa as "infallible" or attribute to him powers of intercession. This is a total distortion of Submitters theology.

### The Rejection of Intercession
Submitters strictly uphold the Quranic verdict that no human being possesses independent intercessory power before God:

<Verse chapter={21} verses="26-28">
Yet, they said, "The Most Gracious has begotten a son!" Glory be to Him. All (messengers) are (His) honored servants. They never speak on their own, and they strictly follow His commands. He knows their future and their past. They do not intercede, except for those already accepted by Him, and they are worried about their own necks.
</Verse>

Messengers do not precede God in speech (*lā yasbiqūnahu bil-qawl*); they cannot advocate or alter divine judgment on the Day of Resurrection. Salvation belongs exclusively to God.

### Human Fallibility vs. Protected Revelation
The Quran records that messengers are fallible human beings capable of mortal shortcomings when acting outside direct divine inspiration:
* **Jonah** left his assignment in frustration and was swallowed by the fish (**37:139–144**).
* **Muhammad** was rebuked for turning away from the blind man (**80:1–10**) and for his words concerning Zaid's wife (**33:37**).
* **David** repented after passing a hasty judgment (**38:24**).
* **Abraham** prayed for his idol-worshipping father until instructed otherwise (**9:114**).

The Quran encapsulates this human reality:

<Verse chapter={34} verses="50">
Say, "If I stray, I stray only to my own loss, and if I am guided, it is because of what my Lord inspires in me. He is Hearer, Near."
</Verse>

While the mortal messenger is human, the **divine message, mathematical proof, and Quranic purification inspired by God** are completely protected. Submitters do not worship the mortal man; they obey the divine commission entrusted to him.

## 8. Evolving Guidance and Community Maturation

Critics often cite Dr. Khalifa's early public debate remarks (such as his 1988 statement, *"Nobody follows me... we follow the word of God alone"*) to allege that his later emphasis on obeying the Messenger of the Covenant was a contradiction.

This argument betrays a fundamental misunderstanding of the Quranic principle of **progressive divine instruction and community maturation**:

### The Sedentary Believers Paradigm
In the Quran, divine directives evolved as the early community matured:
1. In **4:77** and **4:95**, early believers were told they did not have to fight, and that the sedentary believers who stayed home were promised salvation alongside those who mobilized.
2. Later, when the community grew in strength and the divine decree to mobilize was issued, remaining sedentary was no longer permitted. Those who refused to mobilize were condemned as **hypocrites whose hearts were sealed** (**9:81, 9:86**).

Can one accuse Muhammad of "contradicting himself" because an early lenient allowance was updated by a later divine decree? Certainly not. Divine commands adapt to community maturation and shifting circumstances.

### The Progression of Dr. Khalifa's Mission
Dr. Khalifa's ministry followed the exact same Quranic trajectory:
* **Early Phase (1970s–mid 1980s)**: The primary objective was breaking the cultural stranglehold of Hadith and Sunnah, introducing the broad principle of "Quran Alone." In debate settings with traditionalists, he emphasized that truth rests in scripture rather than personal followings.
* **Matured Phase (late 1980s–1990)**: As the foundational principle took hold, individuals began drifting into subjective "Quranism," inventing personal dogmas. God then directed the messenger to formally proclaim his identity as the Messenger of the Covenant (**3:81**) and assert his pedagogical authority to preserve the community from theological fragmentation.<Ref id="3" />

Just as the hypocrites in Medina clung to the earlier sedentary exemption to evade their obligations, polemicists cling to early debate soundbites to evade submitting to the messenger's authorized role.

<Verse chapter={2} verses="13">
When they are told, "Believe like the people who believed," they say, "Shall we believe like the fools who believed?" In fact, it is they who are fools, but they do not know.
</Verse>

## 9. Conclusion: True Submission to God Alone

The false dichotomy between "following the Quran" and "following Rashad" dissolves entirely upon rigorous Quranic examination:

1. **The Quran** is the eternal, immutable, fully detailed word of God Almighty—the sole source of religious law.
2. **The Messenger of the Covenant** was the divine instrument chosen to unveil the Quran's mathematical preservation (74:30), purify the religious practices from human corruption, and provide the authorized pedagogical baseline for modern humanity.
3. **Obedience to the Messenger** is not idolatry; it is the practical test of whether a believer submits to God's divine system or elevates their own intellectual ego above God's signs.

Submission Archives preserves both the sacred text and the complete archival record of the messenger who delivered it, ensuring that future generations remain anchored in **God ALONE**, protected from both traditional superstition and subjective sectarianism.
