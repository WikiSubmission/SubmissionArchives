## 1. The Mathematical Discovery

When the word "Allah" (God) was counted across the entire Quran, the total reached **2,699**—a number that is not a multiple of 19 (2,699 = 19 × 142 + 1).

Further analysis revealed that the final verse of Surah 9 (9:129) contained the solitary extra occurrence of the word "Allah" that broke the entire system. When verses 9:128 and 9:129 were examined in isolation:

1. **Total Quranic Verses**: Removing the two verses brought the total to **6,346** (19 × 334).
2. **Word 'Allah' Count**: The frequency of the word "Allah" became **2,698** (19 × 142).
3. **Sum of Verse Numbers**: The sum of all verse numbers where God is mentioned conformed exactly to multiples of 19.<Ref id="1" />

## 2. Historical Confirmation in Classical Hadith

Remarkably, standard Islamic history itself records that the end of Surah 9 was recognized by early scribes as irregular:

- **Sahih Bukhari (Vol. 6, Book 60, Hadith 4988)** records Zaid ibn Thabit’s explicit testimony: *"I found the last two verses of Surah at-Tawbah with Khuzaimah al-Ansari only, and with no one else."*
- **Ibn Abi Dawud in *Kitab al-Masahif*** and **Jalaluddin Suyuti in *Al-Itqan fi 'Ulum al-Qur'an*** document that these verses violated the established rule requiring two independent written witnesses for every verse accepted into the standard compilation.<Ref id="2" />
