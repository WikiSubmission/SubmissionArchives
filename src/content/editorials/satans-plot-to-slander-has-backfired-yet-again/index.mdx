## The Scriptural Law of Adversarial Insinuation

Surat Al-Hajj explicitly articulates the inevitable conflict between divine proclamation and adversarial interference. Verse 22:52 establishes that every previous messenger encountered fabricated whispers and slanderous additions projected into their discourse:

<Verse
  reference="Quran 22:52"
  text="We did not send before you any messenger or prophet without having the devil interfere with his wishes. God then nullifies what the devil casts, and God solidifies His revelations. God is Omniscient, Most Wise."
/>

This mechanism operates across three distinct historical phases:

1. **The Projection of Slander**: Opponents manufacture sensational allegations designed to distract the public from examining the primary evidence.
2. **The Divine Nullification**: The falsehood is examined under rigorous evidentiary scrutiny, exposing its internal contradictions and forensic baselessness.
3. **The Solidification of Truth**: The failed smear campaign forces objective observers to inspect the original records, resulting in wider verification and deeper conviction.
