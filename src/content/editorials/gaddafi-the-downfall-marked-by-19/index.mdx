In the late 1970s, Libyan leader Muammar Gaddafi was briefly exposed to the mathematical composition of the Quran discovered by Dr. Rashad Khalifa. Yet rather than submitting to God alone, Gaddafi increasingly elevated his own person, creating his "Third Universal Theory" in the Green Book and treating his political decrees as sacred law.

## 1. Early Encounters with the Miracle

Between 1978 and 1982, delegations from Libya and international Islamic conferences took notice of Dr. Khalifa's computerized research on the Quranic initials and the number 19.

For a brief window, the Libyan state press printed editions of the Quran highlighting the mathematical code. However, Gaddafi’s regime quickly sought to co-opt the prestige of the discovery for geopolitical influence rather than genuine religious reformation.<Ref id="1" />

<PullQuote attribution="Historical Reality">
Signs from God are meant to purify belief and establish submission, not serve as geopolitical instruments for worldly rulers.
</PullQuote>

## 2. The Descent into Idolatry

By the mid-1980s, Gaddafi’s regime had fully subordinated the Quran to state ideology:

1. **The Green Book**: Elevated as a mandatory ideological text taught in all Libyan schools.
2. **Cult of Personality**: Public statues, massive portraits, and state slogans glorifying the "Brotherly Leader and Guide of the Revolution."
3. **Suppression of True Reform**: Imprisoning anyone who advocated returning to the Quran alone without state control.

<Verse chapter={79} verses="17-25">

He gathered and proclaimed: 'I am your highest lord.' So God seized him with retribution for the last and the first.
</Verse>

## 3. The End of a Tyrant

On **October 20, 2011**, after 42 years of absolute dictatorship, Muammar Gaddafi was captured in a drainage pipe in Sirte and killed in disgrace. The global broadcast of his humiliating end closely mirrored the fate of historical tyrants documented in scripture:

</Verse>
