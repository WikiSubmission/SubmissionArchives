Advocating God Alone

Quran Study
May 26, 1989
Catherine Robinson
1h 19m

Catherine Robinson leads a study of Sura 72 (Al-Jinn) and Sura 73 (Al-Muzzammil) that expands beyond verse commentary into a defense of the human messenger and the mathematical proof of the Quran. Includes an extended debate on Sura 72:27's gematrical value of 1919, the nature of the 19 code, and practical guidance on night prayer and meditation.
