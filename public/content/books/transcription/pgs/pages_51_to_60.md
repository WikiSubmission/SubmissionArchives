The times of the five Salat prayers were dictated to Abraham, and subsequently observed by Muhammad and his followers. The precise time limit for each Salat is detailed in Quran:

**5. AL-'ISHAA' PRAYER:** This is mentioned by name in 24:58. Its time limit is from the time the twilight disappears until the night is over. See 11:114 (the edges of the night).
