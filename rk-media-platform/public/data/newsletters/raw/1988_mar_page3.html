TO SHEIKH AL AZHAR

There is no doubt that you know full well the consequences of disobeying God and his messenger.. utter destruction, as shown on page 1. The question to you is : Does your conscience allow you to lead a whole nation to utter destruction ? Al-Azhar, the rejector of Qur'an, disobeys God and His messenger by claiming infallibility for the prophets and the power of intercession for Prophet Muhammed. Al-Azhar rejects the Qur'an's completeness by upholding Satan's Hadith & Sunna.

AHMAD SUBHY MANSOUR & RASHAD KHALIFA TO SPEAK BEFORE AMNESTY INTERNATIONAL

Amnesty International, the great, London-based humanitarian organization has arranged a talk by Drs. Ahmad Subhy Mansour and Rashad Khalifa to be given at the University of Arizona February 18,

1988. The subject matter will undoubtedly deal with the mental oppression in the Muhammadan world, where people cannot worship God without setting up Muhammad as an idol besides Him. The event is

to be witnessed by the media, and will probably be picked up by the international news agencies. More details in the coming issue of M.P.
