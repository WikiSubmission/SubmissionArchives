The Quran talks about ONE SACRED MOSQUE, but the idol-worshiping Muslims talk about TWO SACRED MOSQUES!!! They created a Sacred Mosque, out of the prophet Muhammad's tomb, besides "The Sacred Mosque." Is this not idolatry? As shown on Page 3, this goes even against Hadith,

The Quran teaches that the Prophet is dead (39:30), powerless even when he was alive (72:21), totally unaware (5:109),cannot hear anybody (16:21), even if he heard anybody, he can do nothing (35:14),and will disown and be the enemy

Here is a real life demonstration of idolatry committed by the Mohamedans - I reprint it here without comment, from a newspaper that is printed in the U.S.A. in 1988:

ISLAM IN AMERICA, August 15, 1988

Muslim women have the same thirst

To the Editor:

In the matter of spirit and soul men and women

have the same thirst and longing, and deserve the same right and opportunity to satisfy them.

When we, Muslim women, go to the Prophet’s mosque in Madinah we too, just as our brothers, yearn for an opportunity to visit the tomb of the holy Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him. But the time allotted for us, in the morning and after the noon prayers, is over before a small number of us have an opportunity even to file past the tomb.

After covering, sometimes, over 1000 kms to reach Madinah, it is a terrible disappointment to go back to our places of residence without this humble desire having been fulfilled. I appeal to the government of the Custodian of the two Holy Mosques, King Fahd, to increase the time set apart for women.

Mrs. Rukhsana Shafqat Taif
