Polygamy

Continued from page 1

1.   If it must be done, polygamy must alleviate pain and suffering, and not create any pain or suffering for any sincere submitter to God and His laws. Thus, you must ask yourself: If I practice polygamy, will this hurt my present wife and/or children? Will I generate pain and suffering for them? If the answer is “Yes,” you had better re-consider polygamy, and concentrate your efforts and attention on

your present wife and children.

2.   If you and your present family are still young, chances are you will be abusing God’s law if you practice polygamy. Some men come to me and say: “My wife is frigid, and cannot satisfy my drive.” First of all, there is no such thing as a “frigid wife.” Instead of blaming the wife’s frigidity, I ask the husband to exert more loving attention to his wife. Invariably, the wife becomes un-frigid and the marriage is restored by God to a great joy.

3.   You cannot substitute a younger, “more exciting” model

for your wife. This will tell you: you are a slave of your lusts. The mark of a true believer is self-discipline and self-control. If you practice polygamy because of lust, the retribution will be severe and just not worth it. Worse yet, you will anger God and bring a lot of misery upon yourself and your new “wife.”

          Abusing God’s law is a gross offense, and you cannot trick or deceive God. You better examine the                circumstances, and examine yourself very carefully before practicing polygamy.
