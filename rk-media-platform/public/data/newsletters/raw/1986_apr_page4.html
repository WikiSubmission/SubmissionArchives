FIRST INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE OF THE UNITED ISLAMIC NATION
SAN FRANCISCO, AUGUST 29, 30, & 31
(PRELIMINARY DAILY PROGRAM)

5.00-6.00 Fajr Prayer followed by a talk.
6.00-7.00 Announcements.
7.00-8.00 Breakfast.
8.00-12.00 Lectures.
12.00-4.00 Noon (or Friday) Prayer followed by lunch break.
4.00-5.00 Afternoon Prayer followed by talk.
5.00-6.00 Open Forum. Questions & Answers.
6.00-7.00 Dinner break.
7.30-9.00 Sunset Prayer followed by talk.
9.00 Night Prayer followed by Qur’anic study and open forum.

Subjects of the talks will cover DECLARATION OF GOD’S KINGDOM (which begins Muharram 1st, 1407 [September 6, 1986]) ACCORDING TO QUR’AN, REVIEWING LAST YEAR’S EVENTS, FUTURE PROJECTIONS, BASIC BELIEFS, PURIFICATION OF ISLAM THROUGH REMOVAL OF ALL INOVATIONS, OUTSTANDING LECTURES BY DR. AHMAD SUBHY MANSOUR, SOUSSAN REY, DR. RASHID HAMED, IHSAN RAMADAN, RAYMOND CATTON, MEHRI PEROUZ, MAHMOUD PEROUZ, SOPHIE CATTON, ISMAIL BARAKAT, FEROUZ KARMALLY, MALIK ALI, RASHAD KHALIFA, & OTHERS. FULL PARTICIPATION OF ALL ATTENDEES THROUGH OPEN FORUMS.

REPORTS WILL BE PRESENTED FROM OUR COMMUNITIES IN AMERICA’S EAST COAST, WEST COAST, THE MIDWEST, THE NATION’S CAPITAL, EGYPT, EUROPE, AFRICA, ETC.

Attendance is by invitation only. Attendees will arrange and pay for their travel expenses, and their room and board at the conference. Hotel accommodations will be available to many attendees. As you know, God will generously reimburse you manyfold.
