GOOD NEWS TO ALL MUSLIMS

GOD SENDS NEW
OVERWHELMING MIRACLE

GUARANTEED TO CONVERT
THE WHOLE WORLD

A NEW Quranic miracle has been discovered that is guaranteed to convince the whole world, sooner or later, that the Quran is God’s message to the world. In accordance with 41:53, the Quran’s 19-based mathematical miracle has been snowballing. With this report, the miracle has become irresistibly overwhelming.

This is to report an awesome Quranic discovery by Brother Feroz Karmally of Canada.

In a previous issue of the MUSLIM PERSPECTIVE, I reported the fantastic discovery by Brother Abdullah Arik; a discovery that proves that every single verse is divinely fixed far beyond the abilities of all the humans and all the jinns combined.

Here is Arik’s discovery once more: If you write down the number of every verse in the Quran, preceded by the number of verses in each sura, you end up with a very long number consisting of 12692 digits. For example, Sura 1 consists of 7 verses. So you write down 7, followed by the number of every constituent verse in this

sura. Thus, the number representing Sura Al-Faatehah will look like this: 71234567.

Sura 2, Al-Baqarah (The Heifer) consists of 286 verses. Therefore, the number representing Sura 2 will look like this: 286123456……..285286.

The number that covers Suras 1 and 2 will look like this: 71234567286123456……….285286.

After you carry out this process for all suras, you end up with a very long number consisting of 12692, or 19x668, digits.

The number itself, which will take hundreds of pages to write down, is a multiple of 19. Any person with a computer can verify this miraculous physical fact. The computer program is very easy; it take one number at a time, divides it by 19, then

carries the remainder to the next number, and so on. For example, the first numbers in the 12692-digit complete number are: 71234567286123456.……… The program takes 7, finds that it is not divisible by 19, so it takes 71, finds that it equals 19x3 + 14. The computer then puts 14 next to the 2, divides 142 by 19, and so on to the end of the Quran.

Feroze Karmally was demonstrating this awesome miracle to a friend, using his computer. In the process of showing his friend, he got an idea. Why not put the number of verses in each sura at the end of the list of verse numbers? Thus, the data for Sura 1 will be 12345677 instead of 71234567.
