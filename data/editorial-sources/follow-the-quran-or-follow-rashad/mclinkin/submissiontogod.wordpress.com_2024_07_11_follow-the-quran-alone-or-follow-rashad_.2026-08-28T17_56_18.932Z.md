The Quran says that it is fully detailed and provides examples of everything we need for religious guidance. Thus the Quran cites stories of messengers and stories of Muhammad to serve as past precedents that guide us in the modern time. One such lesson is the principle of evolving divine guidance and commands as a result of evolving circumstances and community maturation over time. This principle of evolution and adaptation in divine commands and guidance is crucial for understanding and applying the Quran and the teachings of recent messengers like Rashad Khalifa. The relevant example is the status of the sedentary Submitters.

**\[Quran 4:95\]** Not equal are the sedentary among the believers who are not handicapped, and those who strive in the cause of God with their money and their lives. God exalts the strivers with their money and their lives above the sedentary. For **both**, God promises salvation, but God exalts the strivers over the sedentary with a great recompense.

Here, the sedentary who are not disabled are told that they are promised salvation along with those who mobilize and strive. They could stay at home if they want to and not mobilize with the believers for war and for the cause of God. Yet, later on, they were told if they don’t mobilize, they are going to hell and they are hypocrites if they choose not to mobilize:

**\[Quran 9:81\]** The sedentary rejoiced in their staying behind the messenger of God, and hated to strive with their money and their lives in the cause of God. They said, “Let us not mobilize in this heat!” Say, “The fire of Hell is much hotter,” if they could only comprehend.

**\[Quran 9:86\]** When a sura is revealed, stating: “Believe in God, and strive with His messenger,” even the strong among them say, “Let us stay behind!. They chose to be with the sedentary. Consequently, their **hearts were sealed**, and thus, they cannot comprehend.

Additionally, Muhammad had told them early on that they don’t have to fight, all they need to do is observe the Contact Prayers and pay the obligatory charity:

**\[Quran 4:77\]** Have you noted those who were told, “You do not have to fight; all you need to do is observe the Contact Prayers _(Salat)_ and give the obligatory charity _(Zakat)_,” then, when fighting was decreed for them, they feared the people as much as they feared God, or even more? They said, “Our Lord, why did You force this fighting on us? If only You respite us for awhile!” Say, “The materials of this world are nil, while the Hereafter is far better for the righteous, and you never suffer the slightest injustice.”

In the above verse, who could issue such an authoritative religious command (“you don’t have to fight, all you need to do is pray and give the obligatory charity) other than Muhammad himself? Muhammad had once told them that they don’t have to fight–they can be sedentary if they so choose. And then he changed his tune, requiring them to mobilize and fight.

Can you imagine Muhammad saying to the believers, “no, you don’t have to fight and mobilize with us if you don’t want to. You can just do the prayers and pay zakat, and you are all set; God promises even the sedentary strivers redemption and paradise, but He does exalt the strivers who mobilize with us to higher ranks” and then later on have Muhammad saying “no, you can’t be sedentary, you must mobilize with us or you are going to hell”. What is going on here? Why is Muhammad contradicting himself? A messenger would never do this, would he?

Indeed, it is not a contradiction, but rather a new decree from God as a result of the evolving circumstances–it reflects a clarification of the believers’ role in different circumstances–an update regarding the requirements for the believers: it is not the case that they can stay sedentary forever. Therefore now, God had later clarified that the believers actually must mobilize in specific circumstances (contrary to the earlier statement that being sedentary is perfectly acceptable). Similarly, because God later clarified that Rashad’s role is to explain the Quran, now, we must follow the Quran alone through Rashad’s teachings (which ultimately is the correct understanding of what the Quran says–and so his teachings are what being Quran alone means: following the correct interpretation of the Quran).

Similarly, the command to follow the Quran alone \[ie. your way etc.\] and being only responsible for that and not Rashad’s teachings was a true command at the time…until God issues the clarification, update and new command that Rashad is the one tasked with explaining the Quran and we are to accept those teachings. Thus in order to truly follow the Quran alone, you must not follow your opinion of what the Quran says, but instead, what God teaches us the Quran said through the messenger, Rashad Khalifa.  It’s not a contradiction but a clarification and update on _how_ to follow the Quran alone. Indeed, you are only responsible for what you know–before Rashad issues the statement that his specific teachings on the Quran must be followed and he must be obeyed, you were not necessarily responsible to follow Rashad’s teachings (you could stay _‘sedentary’_), however, after the clarification and knowledge of these new commands, you are now responsible for Rashad’s teachings (you can’t stay sedentary permanently and you must mobilize with us). They were not initially required to accept Rashad’s teachings because Rashad had not given that command yet, thus Rashad’s earlier statements of general following of Quran alone are not false, but instead the earlier requirements of the believers prior to God’s new command–which serves to specify this command further. (We will see later on in the blog, God Willing, that following the wrong interpretation of the Quran (what you think the Quran says) is **not** actually following the Quran alone, you are not following the Quran at this point–to be truly Quran alone, you must follow the **correct interpretation** of the Quran–and God specifies the correct interpretation of the Quran through the Messenger He chose).

With Rashad, the circumstances have changed now that people were beginning to put their own opinion and call it being “Quran alone”, when what they are actually following is their own opinions. Additionally, when Rashad gave the general command of “Quran alone” it was in the context of his teachings against hadith and sunnah. That was the level of maturity of the community–as circumstances evolve–there is a clarification and new command to be Quran alone based on what God reveals to the messenger, rather than your own interpretation of the Quran, which is not in fact the Quran in the first place. It served as an implicit clarification regarding what the Quran really is: not your opinion of the Quran, but the actual objective _meaning_ of the Quran.

Initially, with Muhammad, certain groups were given the impression that they could remain sedentary and attain paradise, later, when the command was clarified, these groups were required to mobilize and those who resisted faced divine rebuke. As the community grew and circumstances changed, the expectations and commands became more stringent, reflecting a maturity in the community’s spiritual and social responsibilities. Muhammad’s stance hardened, especially towards the sedentary, where it was no longer a stance of persuasion and leniency, instead they were given harsh rebuke and they became labeled as **“hypocrites”** in spite of the earlier assurance that being a sedentary believer also guarantees paradise. The experience of Rashad khalifa, mirrors that same progression of leniency, persuasion and general broad commands of following Quran alone to a hardened stance, and a label of the group who fails to adapt to God’s new commands & clarifications as **“hypocrites”**. Thus, what we observe with Rashad Khalifa exhibits a progression that mirrors the Quranic paradigm of evolving divine commands, with the later commands superseding the earlier commands. Indeed, the Quran provides examples of everything we need for religious guidance–these examples are cited for us to see the parallelism and the consistency in the ways of God.

The early Rashad Khalifa initially focused on the fundamental message of following the Quran alone and rejecting hadith as a source of law. He even went as far as saying “you don’t need Rashad, you can just follow the Quran alone. If I say anything not in the Quran, you must reject it”. Here is a direct quote:

_“If you follow Dr. Khalifa, you are not right. Anybody here, and we all know this, we do not follow any person and we emphasize here that we are worshippers of God alone. We follow the word of God alone and if I say anything that is not in the Quran, contradicting the Quran, I am the first to appreciate alerting me to this because then I am wrong. Nobody follows me”._–Rashad Khalifa, Great Debate, July 6, 1988

Later on the tune changed to “you must obey the messenger”, “the Quran misguides without a messenger”, “ **hypocrites** say we don’t need a messenger, we can just worship God alone and follow the Quran alone \[without Rashad’s input\]”. Thus, the messenger’s role was clarified by God–he took on the role of being the one tasked with explaining the Quran, rather than just a limited messenger to guide people generally out of hadith into a general Quran alone direction without further guidance. As the community matured and they were persuaded to be Quran alone by God’s grace, and as the community began to be plagued with the beginnings of Quranism where people started putting their opinion of the Quran as being “Quran alone” even if it contradicts Rashad’s statements (an evolving circumstance), now more specific commands are issued regarding Rashad’s role and clarifications regarding what it really means to be Quran alone–not to follow your opinion of what the Quran says, but to instead follow what God teaches us through the messenger.

![](https://submissiontogod.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/hypocrites.png)![](https://submissiontogod.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/hypocrites-2.jpg)

_**Now**_, as we see in the above writings by Rashad, it is actually the **_hypocrites_** who say ‘we don’t need Rashad’. But earlier, Rashad said “we don’t need Rashad, we have the Quran”. What’s going on here? This is almost identical to hypocrites in Muhammad’s time saying “we don’t need to fight, we can stay sedentary and God promises us redemption and paradise just as Muhammad said earlier ” after Muhammad issued the later command that the sedentary are now required to mobilize. Notice the **similarity of the hypocrites**. They were too content with the earlier command, preferred it more, and they failed to adapt to the later command that was issued in response to a change in circumstance and a clarification regarding their obligations. (Rashad was informed of his role as the teacher of the Quran, thus to follow the Quran alone, you must follow the true Quran that God explains through the messenger’s teachings and not your opinion of the Quran).

This also serves as a test of submission for them (whether they unhesitatingly obey God’s commands, even when these commands are updated) and as a means to expose the hypocrites whose loyalty is not completely towards God alone and they instead follow their own desires. In other words, since they were not content with updated commands from God, that means they were hypocrites and truly were only with God because they liked/agreed with God in this instance–when God reveals a new command they didn’t like, they proved that they were not loyal to God in the first place, but instead were loyal to what they liked and agreed with (ie. they were hypocrites and they worshiped their own desires/likings/ego as a god beside God).

Indeed if you ask the submitter hypocrites whether we are fools for following Rashad’s teachings and trusting God’s system of explaining things through the messenger, the hypocrites will say ‘yes’. Even if they don’t directly say it, they internally believe it as we see from their own words and actions. They think we are fools for accepting those teachings of Rashad including the audios, videos and writings. In fact, the Quran informs us that when the hypocrites were told to believe **_the way_** that the believers believe, they will say **“shall we believe like the fools who believed”**:

**\[Quran 2:13\]** When they are told, “Believe like **( _kama_, كَمَآ , _ie. mimicking the way_)** the people who believed,” they say, **“Shall we believe like the fools who believed?”** In fact, it is they who are fools, but they do not know.

**Addendum:**

Again, it should be emphasized that the aspect of ‘not referring to Rashad’ is the only thing that was updated. Initially, they were not required to refer to Rashad–that command was not issued by God at that time–however, later on, that command was issued. The “follow Quran alone” aspect never changed, only further clarified/specified, because Rashad’s teachings ARE Quran alone based and all of them can be derived from the Quran. Rashad’s role shifted to one that explains the Quran. Now with this specific role, the command is now to follow the Quran alone by obeying God’s teachings of the Quran through the messenger. Because Rashad’s role was further specified by God, the command to follow the Quran alone was specified rather than staying generalized.

In other words, they were not initially required to accept Rashad’s teachings because Rashad had not given that command yet, thus Rashad’s earlier statements of ‘general following of Quran alone’ and ‘you don’t need Rashad’ are not false, but instead reflected the earlier commands of God at the time, prior to God’s new command that they _now_ must follow Rashad’s teachings, in response to the evolving community circumstance where they started seeing the beginnings of Quranism develop. Indeed, God’s divine wisdom knew that Quranism would be a problem (in fact, Rashad will say “my people have abandoned the Quran” at the end times–Quranism is an abandonment of the Quran’s objective teachings and instead the following of your own interpretation of the Quran)–and God clearly set this up a a test to expose hypocrites, almost perfectly mirroring the change/update in divine command regarding being sedentary.

Believers must show unhesitating submission and be adaptable to God’s commands. When God initially issues a command, you must accept it wholeheartedly, when God updates or further specifies the command, you must also accept it wholeheartedly.

Just as when Muhammad told them they don’t have to mobilize or strive, all they have to do is pray and give zakat–he didn’t necessarily know that in the future God will update this and require them all to mobilize and fight in the cause of God. Of course some may see this as a contradiction, but in fact, it is not. Similarly, Rashad had assumed his role was as a very general guide and that is part of the reason he did not declare messengership for so long. He genuinely believed (before God updated and further specified his role) he could get away with not proclaiming messengership and just guiding people to a general Quran alone direction. Rashad even stated he never intended on publically declaring messenger hip, even while knowing he was a messenger–it was only when God gave the direct command, and almost fired him for not declaring messengership and threatening him with hell that he finally declared messengership. Rashad Khalifa was also piecing things together himself and he was going through his own gradual growth and transitions similar to Muhammad. Consider Meccan Muhammad vs late Medinian Muhammad, where Muhammad shifted from a persuasive leniency role to a hardened authoritative role. Rashad himself was also working on the earlier paradigm before God’s new commands were issued and while Rashad thought his role was a general persuasive lenient guru meant to get people into a general Quran alone direction and out of Hadith & Sunnah. Following the new commands, Rashad understood his role as being more specific and being tasked with explaining the Quran. Alongside the new understanding, similar to Muhammad’s new understanding that the sedentary are now required to mobilize, Rashad’s attitude changed to being a more hardened and authoritative messenger delivering specific guidance that is to be obeyed. As his understanding of his role evolved, Rashad introduced these new teachings that emphasized the necessity of following him as a messenger. The Quran alone approach is no longer a general ‘follow what you think the Quran says’ but instead, a more specific ‘follow what the Quran _actually_ says’–hence the later necessity to follow Rashad’s explanations.

**Conclusion:**

The Quran exemplifies the principle of evolving divine guidance, with changing commands reflecting new circumstances and stages of community development. This dynamic approach ensures that divine wisdom remains relevant and practical, initially using a lenient and persuasive method as a mercy to facilitate people’s entry into the religion. Once established, it further specifies roles and adds additional requirements. Rashad Khalifa’s progression in his teachings aligns with this Quranic paradigm. His evolving role as a messenger illustrates the similarity in God’s methods, as reflected in the Quranic guidance. God revealed the Quran in stages, gradually updating believers to adapt to new circumstances, underscoring the importance of following divine commands as they are revealed. This principle of evolution and adaptation in divine guidance is crucial for understanding and applying the teachings of both the Quran and recent messengers like Rashad Khalifa.

Thus the answer to the title of this blog is “Follow Quran Alone”, the actual Quran, not your opinion of the Quran’s meaning. We don’t follow Rashad’s understanding of the Quran because we trust his intellect, but because we trust God who promises to guide us if we follow and obey the messenger:

**\[Quran 24:54\]** Say, “Obey GOD, and obey the messenger.” If they refuse, then he is responsible for his obligations, and you are responsible for your obligations. If you obey him, you will be guided. The sole duty of the messenger is to deliver (the message).

The messenger of God, Rashad Khalifa, commanded us to accept his role and his explanations/understandings of the Quran. We must obey this messenger’s command. It’s not because we are loyal or we trust a man, it is because we are loyal and trust in God, who commanded us to do this and created a system for us for us to abide by rather than object to. Similarly Satan was commanded to prostrate to the humans, but he chose not to. If he was loyal to God, he would have prostrated to the humans not because he is loyal to or even liked the humans, but because he is loyal to the Lord of the Universe.

All is for God alone.
