Many people wonder that since we already have the Quran, why do we need a messenger at all? Why would God send someone unnecessary if we already have the full Quran, especially if that messenger doesn’t come with anything new and only tells us to follow the Quran? What’s the point of that?

However, what we see throughout the Quran is that God has given messengers to even monotheist believers who have a complete scripture full of all of their commandments. The one that sticks out the most is Jesus. What’s the point of sending him? The Jews were already monotheists and follow the 617 commandments of the Torah. An outside observer may say “they didn’t need Jesus; they already had the Torah and they are monotheists following all of God’s commandments”. The fact of the matter is God tests the people; they have to be tested– each in a different way. So He sends messengers with additional signs, miracles and revelations they must believe and obey. It’s just part of the system.

The Quran speaks of many messengers coming to the Jews, and many they Jews killed. Messengers are supposed to come with signs, miracles and tests—and they are supposed to cause division and animosity. They come with something that the people find disturbing and don’t like—and this gives them the leeway to disbelieve. It’s just another part of the life test.

When you have been indoctrinated as a child to believe in a certain religion, you will believe in it. That is not impressive. Anyone can do that. Children can be raised up to believe in anything, they are extremely suggestive, and they can be brought up to believe in anything and if this upbringing is strong enough, these beliefs will crystallize, and they will hold on to them with such conviction into adulthood. Do you think God won’t take into account that you were given an inherent advantage of being born to a religious family and being raised into the right religion? You don’t earn credits for this. This is not something you earned; it doesn’t count. You only get credit for what you earn. This is why you must be tested; this is why God says He will test all the believers. And this can take on the form of personal revelations, signs, or guidance from God to you to see if you would accept them, ignore them, disbelieve them or turn away from them arrogantly. The test can also be in the form of giving counterevidence that makes you disbelieve in your religion or God. And this test can also take the form of new messengers or revelations as a test for you, even if you are already a monotheist on the right path. This is a mechanism to make those whose hearts have a disease disbelieve. It’s not impressive that they believe because they were raised into the right religion. That is meaningless. It’s not worth anything. We see throughout nature and the study of science, that God is extremely measured, and His thoughts and creations are exceptionally complicated—this impressive all intelligent mind can take all things into account simultaneously. He has given humans the ability to use mathematics in Statistics to create complex equations that accounts for and normalizes the different circumstances into one standardized calculation. Do you think God is not able to normalize people’s different circumstances and measure their righteousness into one standardized calculation that takes into account all of their different individual circumstances? Every little detail will be accounted for in the “equations” of God that measure your righteousness.  And this includes the fact that being born into the right religion doesn’t count as credit for you. You must be tested to see if you are actually good enough to enter God’s kingdom. Being born into the right religion is not a free pass; you only get credit for what you earn.

With regards to Idol worship, how would God test people who were raised as children to not believe in other deities other than God to see if they would renounce their idol worshiping tendencies? By testing them with non-deities that they can idolize. Indeed, the Quran informs us that there are many types of non-deities people can take as gods alongside God, and that even includes their own selves and their desires (see Supplement section below). And yet despite this, many still insist that idol worship is only limited to the literal worship of other deities. In fact, Idol worship can be as subtle as focusing and reverencing others besides God. Here is a quote from someone who is a Traditional Muslim from the discord server who refused to accept the message of Submission:

“Muhammad The Final Prophet, The Light of this World, The Obliterator and the first intercessor ﷺ is God’s mouth-piece on Earth, and he is to be obeyed without hesitation”

This is extreme reverence for someone alongside God. What do you expect people with idol worshiping tendencies (diseased hearts) do when they are raised into a religion that tells them there is no other deity other than God? They will find some loophole to worship and reverence non-deities alongside God. They will try to elevate and exalt and reverence another non-deity alongside God and find whatever loophole and justification they can to satisfy their intrinsic idol worshiping lust; to satisfy their diseased hearts. They cannot say that Muhammad is a god because it would contradict the religious beliefs their parents raised them to hold. And so they will incorporate whatever subtle idol worship they can find with the limited framework they have and this idolatry will be subtle enough that they can assert plausible deniability that what they do is not Idol worship. Do they think they can fool God? Does God not take everything into account in His final “equations” & calculations of their righteousness? Is God going to overlook the fact they are idolizing Muhammad because they couldn’t squeeze in another deity based on the framework of religious beliefs they grew up to believe? The idol worshiping soul will want to seek whatever Idol it can, but because it has a limited framework to work with in the religion it was born into, it will seek whatever perceived loophole they can find. A soul that desires Idol worship but has been raised in an Islamic framework that is so against Idol Worship, will find whatever sliver of idol worship they can get away with while thinking they can plausibly deny it as idol worship. Why is focusing and reverencing on God alone not sufficient for them? Why include others beside Him? Even if God blessed others with the messenger-ship and guidance, why reverence them rather than the one who created them that way and guided/elevated them to that position? Even if God gave others His honor and praise, and even if He says these righteous people manifest God’s honor and praise to great degrees (similar to how the magnificence of the physical universe are manifestations of God’s power and praise and capability and excellence), why reverence and praise them rather than God or alongside God? Who does _all_ praise belong to? Why then must you reverence other things alongside God? If God alone is not enough for you to be remembered and reverence with no one else alongside Him, then your heart is still diseased and still unsatisfied with the worship of God alone, and you need to seek refuge and help in God alone to heal you.
