## Part 1: They Never Speak on Their Own
*Examining Quran 21:24–29 and the Context of Intercession*

> **[Quran 21:24]** Have they found other gods beside Him? Say, "Show me your proof. This is the message to my generation, consummating all previous messages." Indeed, most of them do not recognize the truth; this is why they are so hostile.  
> **[Quran 21:25]** We did not send any messenger before you except with the inspiration: "There is no god except Me; you shall worship Me alone."  
> **[Quran 21:26]** Yet, they said, "The Most Gracious has begotten a son!" Glory be to Him. All (messengers) are (His) honored servants.  
> **[Quran 21:27]** **They never speak on their own, and they strictly follow His commands.**  
> **[Quran 21:28]** He knows their future and their past. They do not **intercede**, except for those already accepted by Him, and they are worried about their own necks.  
> **[Quran 21:29]** If any of them claims to be a god beside Him, we requite him with Hell; we thus requite the wicked.

### The Misapplication of 21:27
Verse 21:27 is frequently misused to assert that messengers have no free will, can never utter personal shortcomings, or are robotic entities. However, scripture demonstrates that human messengers can make personal errors when acting outside direct divine revelation:
* **Jonah** abandoned his post and was swallowed by the whale (37:139–144).
* **Muhammad** was corrected when he told Zaid to "keep your wife and reverence God" (33:37), and when he turned away from the blind man (80:1–10).
* **David** repented after realizing he passed a hasty judgment (38:24).
* **Abraham** prayed for his idol-worshipping father until corrected by God (9:114, 60:4).
* **Quran 34:50** explicitly declares: *"Say, 'If I stray, I stray only to my own loss, and if I am guided, it is because of what my Lord inspires in me.'"*

### The True Meaning: Complete Invalidation of Independent Intercession
When read in context with verses 26, 28, and 29, 21:27 is about **intercession**. Those who claim Jesus or Muhammad has independent standing before God to plead on judgment day are refuted. The Arabic (*lā yasbiqūnahu bil-qawl*) means "they do not precede Him in speech"—they cannot initiate speech or advocate independently in the divine court. Messengers are accountable servants who worry about their own souls. You have only one Savior and Protector: God Alone.

## Part 2: Don't Be a Pharisee
*Subtle Idolatry, The Divine Testing System, and Spiritual Maturity*

### The Purpose of Sending Messengers to Monotheists
Many ask: *"If we already have the complete Quran, why do we need a messenger?"*  
The Quran shows that God consistently sends messengers to monotheistic communities who already possess complete scriptures. Jesus was sent to the Jews who already possessed the Torah and its commandments. 

God tests every soul (29:2). Being born into a monotheistic environment or inheriting correct doctrines does not earn righteousness on its own:
> *"Do you think God won't take into account that you were given an inherent advantage of being born into a religious family? You only get credit for what you earn. This is why you must be tested... through new messengers, signs, or obligations to see if you submit to God or your own ego."*

### Subtle Forms of Idolatry
The Quran reveals that idolatry extends far beyond physical idols:
* Taking one's ego/desire as a god (25:43, 45:23)
* Religious scholars and leaders (9:31)
* Upholding religious sources beside God (6:19, 6:114)
* Property, wealth, and children (18:42, 7:190)
* Placing partners with God in praise, fear, or reverence

Traditional religion falls into the trap of the **Pharisees**: obsessing over inherited technicalities and ancestral pride while neglecting absolute devotion to God Alone. When God sends a messenger, it shatters this complacent Pharisaism, forcing believers to prove whether their allegiance is to God or to their personal egos.

> **[Quran 39:45]** When GOD ALONE is mentioned, the hearts of those who do not believe in the Hereafter shrink with aversion. But when others are mentioned beside Him, they become satisfied.

## Part 3: I Wish I Had Followed the Path with the Messenger
*Quran 25:27–28 and the Universal Safety of Obeying Messengers*

> **[Quran 25:27]** The day will come when the transgressor will bite his hands (in anguish) and say, "Alas, I wish I had followed the path with the messenger."  
> **[Quran 25:28]** "Alas, woe to me, I wish I did not take that person as a friend."

### The Quranic Precedent on Misguidance
Throughout the entire Quran, the people of Hell blame various entities for misleading them:
* Tyrants and leaders (20:79: *"Pharaoh misled his people"*)
* Deviant companions and false friends (25:28)
* The Samarian (20:85)
* Scholars, elders, and innovators (7:38, 33:67)
* Wicked Jinns and humans (41:29)

**There is not a single verse, instance, or precedent in the Quran where someone was misled by obeying God's messenger.** Obeying the messenger is universally protected by God as the path of guidance:
> *"Say, 'Obey GOD, and obey the messenger... If you obey him, you will be guided.'"* (Quran 24:54)

## Part 5: Follow the Quran Alone or Follow Rashad?
*Evolving Divine Guidance, The Sedentary Test, and True Quranic Obedience*

### The Principle of Evolving Divine Directives
The Quran demonstrates that divine instructions adapt to the maturity and circumstances of the community:
1. **The Sedentary Believers**:
   * In **4:95**, the sedentary believers who stayed home were initially told: *"For both, God promises salvation."*
   * In **4:77**, believers were told: *"You do not have to fight; all you need to do is observe the Contact Prayers and give the obligatory charity."*
   * Later, when the community matured and circumstances shifted, the decree changed in **9:81** and **9:86**: those who insisted on remaining sedentary were rebuked as **hypocrites whose hearts were sealed**.
2. **The Progression of Rashad Khalifa's Mission**:
   * *Early Phase*: Focused on breaking the shackles of Hadith and Sunnah, establishing the foundational principle of "Quran Alone" in broad terms.
   * *Matured Phase*: As individuals began interpreting the Quran through subjective opinions ("Quranism") and creating their own sectarian dogmas, God clarified the messenger's role as the authorized teacher and clarifier of the Quran (3:81, 75:19, 16:44).

### "Quran Alone" vs. Personal Opinion
Following what you *think* the Quran says based on personal whims is not "Quran Alone"—it is following your own desire. True adherence to the Quran requires accepting the objective divine meaning conveyed by the messenger whom God sent with the proof.

> **[Quran 26:108]** "You shall reverence God and obey me."  
> **[Quran 43:63]** "I bring to you wisdom, and to clarify some of the matters in which you dispute. You shall reverence God and obey me."  
> **[Quran 24:54]** "Say, 'Obey GOD, and obey the messenger.' ... If you obey him, you will be guided."

We do not follow a messenger out of worship of a mortal man, but out of total devotion to **God**, who established the institution of messengership as His divine system.
