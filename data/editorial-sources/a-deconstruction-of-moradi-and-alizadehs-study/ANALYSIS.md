*A Detailed Evaluation of "The Numerical Miraculousness of the Qur'an: Evaluating Rashad Khalifa's 52 Claims"*

**Paper Details:**
- **Authors:** Mostafa Moradi (Assistant Professor, Shahid Beheshti University, Tehran) & Maliheh Alizadeh Baygi (Master of Qur'an and Hadith Sciences, Shahid Beheshti University).
- **Publication:** *Journal of Interdisciplinary Qur'anic Studies*, Vol. 2, Issue 2, December 2023, pp. 5–32.
- **Subject:** A critical review of the 52 claims presented in Dr. Rashad Khalifa's 1982 book, *Qur'an: Visual Presentation of the Miracle*.

## 1. The Core Methodological Fallacy: Atomistic Isolation vs. Cumulative Proof

### The Strawman of Standalone Evaluation
Moradi and Alizadeh evaluate all 52 numbered items in *Qur'an: Visual Presentation of the Miracle* as isolated, discrete units ("Claim 1," "Claim 2," etc.), repeatedly applying the refrain:
> *"this specific instance... does not constitute a miraculous or superhuman feature on its own."*

This methodology fundamentally misunderstands the architecture of Dr. Khalifa's work:
1. **Cumulative Architecture**: The book is organized from start to finish as an accumulating case where every item is labeled **"PHYSICAL FACT NUMBER (n)"**.
2. **Two-Tier System**: In the concluding section (*Summary & Conclusions*), Khalifa classifies the 52 facts into two labeled tiers:
   - **"The Simple Facts" (1–22)**: Foundational structural parameters (the Basmalah, total chapters, first and last revelations, word counts of the Basmalah).
   - **"The Intricate Facts" (23–52)**: Initial letter frequencies across the 29 initialed chapters (*Muqatta'at*).
3. **Interlocking Chain**: Fact #1 (the 19 letters of the Basmalah) is the foundational root from which Facts 2–22 branch. Facts 14–20 specifically track the occurrences of the exact four words of Fact #1 (*Ism*, *Allah*, *Al-Rahman*, *Al-Rahim*).

Evaluating each fact in isolation and concluding that no single fact is miraculous on its own is an elementary category error. A brick is not a cathedral, but dismissing the cathedral by testing individual bricks in isolation is fallacious.

## 2. The Chiasmic Symmetry of Revelations (Refuting the Claim 9 Critique)

In evaluating **Claim 9** (the first verse of Surah al-Nasr = 19 letters), the authors object:
> *"why focus on letters and not words? This claim exemplifies highly subjective and non-general claims..."*

This critique completely misses the deliberate **mirrored bookend structure** spanning Facts 3 through 13:

| Revelation Event | Word Count | Letter Count | Verse Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First Chronological Revelation** (96:1–5) | **19 words** (Fact 3) | **76 letters** = $19 \times 4$ (Fact 4) | Sura 96 has **19 verses** (Fact 5); Sura 96 has **285 letters** = $19 \times 15$ (Fact 7) |
| **Last Chronological Revelation** (Sura 110) | **19 words** (Fact 8) | **19 letters** in Verse 1 (Fact 9) | Whole Sura = 3 verses; 1st verse = 19 letters |

Khalifa did not arbitrarily switch from words to letters; he documented both word-counts and letter-counts across both the alpha and omega revelations. The authors' atomized format obscures the intentional structural symmetry.

## 3. The Fatal Concession: Improbability Acknowledged, Never Computed

In Section 4, the authors define four necessary criteria for establishing a numerical miracle, explicitly including **Criterion 3: "Probability of Randomness is Close to Zero"**.

However, in Section 6.50 (evaluating Claims 38–50 on the disjointed letter systems), the authors make a remarkable admission:
> *"Although the repetition of these letters in these Surahs being multiples of 19 does not constitute a numerical miracle due to its lack of generality across all Surahs containing disjointed letters, **if more precise calculations were performed, the improbability of such a coincidence would be significant.**"*

And in Section 7:
> *"the selection of the number 19 by Rashad Khalifa is considered a significant innovation and that the correct claims indicate the possibility of intentional connections around the number 19 in the Qur’an by Allah."*

### The Methodological Contradiction:
1. The authors mandate probability calculation as a core criterion.
2. They concede in print that the joint accumulation of 19-multiples across disjointed-letter chapters is statistically improbable by chance.
3. **Yet nowhere in the entire 28-page study do the authors perform a single probability calculation.**

By declining to compute the joint binomial/multinomial probability across the 29 initialed chapters ($P < 10^{-15}$), the authors fail to execute their own stated methodology.

## 4. The "Lack of Generality" Critique vs. Textual Reality

27 of the 52 claims were rejected by the authors under the classification of *"arbitrary and lacks generality."*

This critique collapses when compared to the physical realities of the Quranic text:
* The disjointed letters (*Muqatta'at*) are inherently heterogeneous: 14 unique Arabic letters appearing in 14 distinct sets at the beginning of 29 chapters.
* Single-letter initials (e.g. *Qaf* in Surah 50, *Nun* in Surah 68) naturally operate at the chapter level.
* Multi-chapter initial groups (e.g. *A.L.M.* across 6 chapters, *H.M.* across 7 chapters, *A.L.R.* across 5 chapters) naturally operate as interconnected linguistic families.
* The groupings are not post-hoc selections by modern analysts; they are fixed, immutable physical headings established in the 7th century.

## 5. Specific Computational and Textual Clarifications

   * Khalifa's complete computer concordance details all 2,698 occurrences of the root *Allāh* across the 114 chapters.
   * The authors' claim of discrepancies stems from conflating standard orthographic variants (*Allāhumma*, which appears 5 times and is counted distinctly) with the primary noun forms.
2. **Claim 28 (The Letter Nūn in Sura 68 = 133 = $19 \times 7$):**
   * The opening initial of Surah 68 is written in the original Uthmanic codex spelled phonetically as *Nūn-Waw-Nūn* (نـون), yielding 133 Nūns ($19 \times 7$).
3. **Claim 51 (The 14-14-29 Structural Identity = 57 = $19 \times 3$):**
   * 14 unique initial letters + 14 unique initial combinations + 29 initialed chapters = $57 = 19 \times 3$.
   * The authors dismiss this as "arbitrary," yet these three parameters constitute the complete mathematical set of the Quran's initial system.
