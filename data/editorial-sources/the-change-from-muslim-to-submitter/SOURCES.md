# Sources: The Change From "Muslim" to "Submitter"

## The bulletins

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `newsletters/1989_08_August.pdf` | *Muslim Perspective*, August 1989, No. 56. The last issue under the original masthead. |
| `newsletters/1989_09_September.pdf` | *Submission Perspective*, September 1989, No. 57, Safar 1410. Page one carries the editorial "Why the name change?", the source of every quotation in section 2. |
| `newsletters/1990_01_January.pdf` | *Submitters Perspective*, January 1990, No. 61, Jumada II 1410. The first issue under the final masthead, and the last Dr. Khalifa edited. |

The published figure `masthead-sequence.jpg` is composed from page 1 of these three files, rendered at 2.4x and cropped to the masthead block of each.

The full run behind the table in section 1 is the 64-issue corpus (February 1985 to March 1990) in `data/catalog/newsletters.json`, whose `collection_metadata.publication_title_history` records all three titles. Only the three issues that bracket the changes are duplicated here.

## The study session

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `transcripts/quran-study-20-...-insurance-fear-worry.json` | Quran Study 20, Tucson, with Dr. Mahmoud Sabahi leading. Cues from roughly 59:00 to 61:30 carry the exchange quoted in section 3: a member's argument that "the word 'Muslim' may introduce an element of prejudice," and Khalifa's reply naming the earlier title *The Perpetual Miracle of Muhammad*, the effect of "Khomeini and Gaddafi and Islamic revolutionaries" on the word, and the change being made in the final translation. |

The session is undated in the studio catalogue. Internal evidence places it after the September 1989 masthead change (Khalifa reports the bulletin rename in the past tense) and during the preparation of the 1989 translation (which he describes in the present tense).

## The outside witness

FBI Los Angeles teletype FBILA 0005, 5 February 1990, page three, records that the "Moslem Perspective" was "subsequently renamed the 'Submitters Perspective.'" The document is held under the Gaddafi slug, which cites the same page for the Libyan detention.
