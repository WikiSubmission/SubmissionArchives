## United Nations Security Council

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `un-security-council/unsc-resolution-1970-2011-libya-s-res-1970.pdf` | S/RES/1970 (2011), adopted at the **6491st meeting**, 26 February 2011. UN document symbol 11-24558. Twenty-eight operative paragraphs. |
| `un-security-council/unsc-resolution-1973-2011-libya-s-res-1973.pdf` | S/RES/1973 (2011), adopted at the **6498th meeting**, 17 March 2011, by ten votes to none with five abstentions. UN document symbol 11-26839. Twenty-nine operative paragraphs. |

Cited for footnotes 5 and 6, and for the strongest item in section 7: the meeting serial
number 6498 = 19 × 342. The Council has numbered its meetings continuously since January
1946, so the figure is a running count rather than a chosen endpoint. The verbatim record
of the meeting is S/PV.6498, held upstream.

The 6491st meeting is recorded here alongside it because the article's claim depends on
the contrast: 6491 is not a multiple of nineteen, so the multiple falls on the
authorisation of force and not on the sanctions resolution three weeks earlier.
