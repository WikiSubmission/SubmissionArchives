Placement

Ninth ribbon entry, then a tab

The icon closes the top ribbon group under Media Notes. Clicking it opens a tab on the sentinel path qc://workspace, so Ctrl+\ puts the surface in one half and the note in the other, and pane sizes persist per archive with no new code.

Mode selectors

In the footer, not in settings

Text mode and value system sit permanently at the base of the query pane. There is no single true letter count, so the mode is part of every question asked rather than a preference buried elsewhere.

Toggles

Hamza is a checkbox, not a constant

The four toggles mirror the old app's checkbox row, driven by data in text_modes.json. Flip them and every figure in the readout recomputes. Nothing in Rust knows what a hamza is.

Provenance

No number without its convention

The strip under the verse carries mode, value system, scope and verification state. Where a mode has an unresolved rule, the affected figure is marked rather than presented as authoritative.

Colour

Gold means the number, terracotta means you

The accent marks selection. --ed-gold marks a numeric property such as divisibility. In a tool whose whole subject is divisibility, conflating the two would make every hit look like a click.

Segmentation

Prefix, stem and suffix, derived

Hover cards show the word split by walking its root through the consonant skeleton. That aligns 98.84% of rooted words with no morphology file imported, giving 156 prefix and 198 suffix forms as data.
